Questions we often hear

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can PPC help with a brand-new packaging design?

Yes. PPC Flex supports packaging design and prototypes, including the development process for new formats and ideas.

What kinds of packaging does PPC offer?

Solutions include custom bags and pouches, rollstock, reclosable packaging and cleanroom packaging stock products.

Do you offer more sustainable packaging?

PPC Flex offers recyclable and compostable material options. The right choice depends on the product and its performance requirements.
